How the Rhythm Battles Work

The controls are approachable, but consistent timing matters more as songs become faster and patterns grow denser.

Follow the Notes

Watch the incoming arrows and press the matching direction when a note reaches its target. Long notes may need to be held, while rapid sequences reward reading ahead instead of reacting to one arrow at a time.

←↓↑→WASD

Protect Your Momentum

Clean streaks help maintain control of the battle. When a chart becomes difficult, focus on the rhythm and recurring patterns rather than tapping faster than the song demands.

Quick Tips for Better Runs

A few habits can make difficult songs easier to read without changing the basic controls.

Listen before you rushThe instrumental and vocals often hint at when dense note sequences are about to begin.
Read patterns, not single arrowsRecognizing repeated shapes reduces the amount of last-second reaction needed.
Keep a steady hand positionUse the same comfortable keys throughout a song so your fingers do not have to reposition.
Replay difficult sectionsFamiliarity with a song's transitions can matter as much as raw reaction speed.
