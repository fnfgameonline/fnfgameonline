## Welcome to the World of FNF Game

**FNF Game**, also known as Friday Night Funkin', is a music-based rhythm game where timing, creativity, and musical expression come together. Players take on exciting rhythm battles by following note patterns and matching their inputs to the beat. Each encounter introduces different songs, characters, and musical styles, creating an experience that rewards both concentration and a good sense of rhythm.

Whether you are discovering the game for the first time or returning for another musical challenge, FNF offers an engaging environment filled with colorful visuals, memorable tracks, and entertaining gameplay.

## Explore the Main Features

* **Rhythm-Based Gameplay:** Follow scrolling arrows and press the corresponding keys at the right moment to maintain your performance throughout each song.
* **Unique Characters:** Meet recognizable characters with different personalities, visual designs, and musical styles that bring every battle to life.
* **Music and Soundtracks:** Experience a diverse collection of energetic tracks, each featuring distinctive rhythms, melodies, and increasing levels of complexity.
* **Creative Mods:** Explore community-created modifications that introduce original songs, new opponents, redesigned environments, and alternative gameplay experiences.
* **Progressive Challenges:** Improve your timing and coordination as you encounter faster sequences, complex note patterns, and demanding musical battles.

## How to Get Started

1. Choose a song or available game mode that matches your experience level.
2. Pay attention to the incoming arrows and learn how each note corresponds to a keyboard input.
3. Follow the rhythm carefully and press the correct keys when the notes reach their targets.
4. Practice challenging sections to improve accuracy, consistency, and reaction speed.
5. Explore additional songs and mods to discover more musical experiences.
